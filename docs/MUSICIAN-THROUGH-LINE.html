Musician through-line - what happens today

Retrack This · as of Sept 21, 2026 · charge-upfront Checkout live
Companion to the producer through-line. For founder / Damian meetings and community email.
Yellow boxes = open product questions / gaps to discuss.

One-sentence version. Musician signs up → sets payouts in Settings → browses Open jobs → listens Part/Bed/Both → submits takes for free (need payouts ready) → waits through the deadline → if awarded, gets paid and masters unlock; if not, nothing is owed and they can keep browsing.

1. Status pills (what the musician sees on My submissions)

PillMeaning
PendingJob still Open (or in the post-deadline award window). Submission is in; waiting.
PickedProducer favorited this submission before/at award time. Not paid yet until the job is Awarded.
SelectedJob Awarded and this take won. Money moved (or queued for manual PayPal/Wise). Masters unlock.
Not selectedJob Awarded to someone else. No payout for this submission.
Job cancelledProducer cancelled / refunded, or cron refunded with no winner. No payout.

Open jobs on the browse board are the only gigs musicians can see and join. Drafts and unpaid jobs stay invisible.

2. Happy path (decision tree)

START: Musician creates an account (email or Google) │ ├─ Settings → Payouts │ ├─ Stripe Connect (supported countries) → bank onboarding in a new tab │ └─ PayPal / Wise (alt) where Connect isn’t available or preferred │ → required before submit is allowed │ ├─ Instruments (optional but helps matching emails) │ ├─ Browse Open jobs │ ├─ Listen: Part / Bed / Both │ ├─ Read brief, price, deadline, tempo │ │ │ └─ Submit (free) │ ├─ Up to 3 audio takes + optional notes / MIDI │ ├─ One submission per job (can replace files while Open) │ └─ Producer may get a “new take” email (if prefs on) │ └─ After deadline (submissions locked): ├─ Producer awards (or cron auto-awards a favorite) │ → Selected → Stripe transfer or pending_manual_payout │ → “You were selected” email → masters download │ ├─ Someone else wins │ → Not selected (no pay) │ └─ No favorite / cancel / refund path → Job cancelled email if they had submitted

4. Branch: cannot submit yet

Payouts not ready. Submit is blocked until Stripe Connect is ready or PayPal/Wise is on file. Settings → Payouts; Stripe opens in a new tab.
Wrong instrument / closed job. Only Open jobs accept takes. After the deadline, the form is gone even if the producer still has 48h to award.

5. Branch: while waiting

  • Replace files while the job is still Open (before deadline / before award).
  • Favorite is private to the producer - musicians don’t see who is favorited.
  • No early close. Jobs run the full deadline so the submit window is honest.
  • Previews stream as lighter MP3s; full WAV masters unlock only for the winner after Awarded.

6. Branch: payout

  • Platform fee ~10% comes out of the job price; musician gets the rest.
  • Stripe Express: automatic transfer when Connect is ready.
  • PayPal / Wise: job shows Awarded, payment pending_manual_payout until a founder pays by hand.
  • Self-award (same account posting and winning) is blocked.
Musician email on award shared with producer? Still TODO - producers don’t automatically get the winner’s contact email in-product yet.
If the musician isn’t happy after winning? No self-serve reverse. Support / admin only. License to the producer kicks in on award (see Terms).

7. What producers do that musicians feel

  • Pay up front - so the gig is real money when it goes Open.
  • Favorite (one at a time) until deadline; Award only after deadline (or cron).
  • Cancel & refund anytime before award → job cancelled for submitters.
