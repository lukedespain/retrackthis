Hi —

Thanks for being early with us. We’ve been listening to how you’ve been using Retrack This, and a lot of what you’re about to see came straight from that feedback.

The big change is how payment works. When a producer posts a job, they pay up front now. We dropped the old “hold a card for a week” approach — it was confusing, and it put everyone on a clock that didn’t match how people actually finish a gig. Funds sit with us until someone is awarded, and if a job is cancelled or nobody wins, the producer gets refunded.

Jobs also stay open for the full deadline you set. You can favorite a take while submissions are still coming in, but awarding happens after the deadline. You get a short window (about 48 hours) to pick someone. If you’ve already favorited a take and don’t click in time, we’ll award that favorite for you. If you never favorited anyone, the job closes and we refund.

For musicians: submitting is still free. Set up payouts in Settings before you submit so we know where to send money if you win. Your submission stays pending until the job ends — then it’ll show as awarded, not selected, or cancelled. We won’t flash a “favorited” status at you mid-job; that was something people told us would feel stressful, and we agreed.

There’s more detail on the FAQ if you want it, and the Terms and Privacy pages are up to date too:

https://retrackthis.com/faq  
https://retrackthis.com/terms  
https://retrackthis.com/privacy  

If something still feels off, just reply to this email. We’re small enough that we actually read it.

Thanks again for helping us shape this.

— Luke
